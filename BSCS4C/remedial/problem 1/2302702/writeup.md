To build the inventory robustly without crashing on bad data, build_inventory iterates through the list of raw dictionaries and passes each one into Product.from_dict(). The dictionary parsing and object instantiation are wrapped inside a try...except InventoryError block.
A "failure" is defined as any row where the Product constructor fails validation due to invalid business rules (such as a price less than or equal to 0, or a negative quantity). By specifically catching InventoryError rather than using a bare except statement, we ensure that expected data validation issues are gracefully logged into the failures list as {"row": dict, "error": message} without silencing unexpected runtime or system errors. Valid rows are successfully appended to the products list.
